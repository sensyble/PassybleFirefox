## 10. How to sign

Sign before your first pull request is merged; the agreement then covers all your later Contributions too.

* On your pull request, add a comment with exactly this sentence:
  "I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA"
  The CLA check on the pull request asks for it and records your GitHub user name, the comment and its date as
  your signature. Each Passyble repository keeps its own record, so another repository may ask once more.
* If you can't sign on GitHub, e-mail the sentence "I have read the Sensyble Individual Contributor License
  Agreement (CLA.md) and I agree to it.", with your full name, your GitHub user name and the e-mail address of
  your commits, to support@passyble.com with the subject "CLA".

For a corporate agreement (contributions made for an employer), e-mail support@passyble.com with the subject
"Corporate CLA".
