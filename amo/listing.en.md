## Description

Passyble fills your passwords, two-factor codes, passkeys, cards and addresses, right in Firefox.

Keep your vault where it suits you:

* With the Passyble app on your computer. The app holds the vault, unlocks with Windows Hello, and syncs.
* In Firefox itself. No app needed: the vault is encrypted with your master password and stays in this browser.

What you get:

* Logins filled from a menu in the field, or with Ctrl+Shift+L
* Strong passwords made for new accounts, and saved for you
* Two-factor codes filled as you sign in
* Cards and addresses filled into checkout forms
* A lock after the time you pick, and when Firefox closes

By default Passyble only checks account.passyble.com every few hours for the minimum supported version, and sends nothing about you or your vault. Signing in to a Passyble account or turning on sync asks first.

Free. Open source.
